# Audit figures

Contact sheets for the pHash threshold decision (ADR-0005). They were generated by
`ml_research/scripts/audit/make_pair_sheets.py --raw data/raw/kaggle_kebede --out <folder>` on the full dataset with
the ADR-0005 settings (duplicate threshold 4, group threshold 10, reviewed list applied). Each `.jpg`
has a `.csv` that lists the same pairs.

- `pairs_at_5-6.jpg`: all 9 pairs of originals at pHash distance 5–6 (none is at 5). Pairs 7 and 9
  are the reviewed same-field pairs in `ml_research/configs/data/reviewed_duplicates.yaml`.
- `cross_split_d12.jpg`: 20 random cross-split pairs (seed 42) at distance 12, out of 178.

Image credit: T. Y. Rahman, L. B. Mahanta, A. K. Das, J. D. Sarma, "Histopathological imaging
database for Oral Cancer analysis", Mendeley Data, version 2, doi:10.17632/ftmp4cvtmb.2, licensed

public-API dataset record on 22 Nov 2026. Changes: the images were downsized (longest side 360 px)
and arranged side by side with captions.
